**Title:** I made a free Palworld breeding calculator updated for 1.0 (all 299 Pals, every combo, no ads walls or accounts)

**Body:**
Hey everyone. I got tired of calculators that were still missing the 1.0 Pals, so I built my own: https://palworld-breeding-nu.vercel.app/

What it does:
- Pick two parents, see the child instantly (all 44,850 combinations, including the Katress/Wixen gender rule)
- Pick the Pal you want and see every parent pair that makes it, with an optional "must include this parent" filter
- A page for every Pal with its partner skill, the easiest parent pairs, and the shortest breeding route from early-game Pals
- Passive skills list with the in-game rank colours
- Works on phones, dark and light mode, loads in under a second

Data is from the v1.0 breeding table and checked against paldb. If you find a wrong result, tell me the pair and what hatched and I'll fix it.
