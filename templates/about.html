About

Pal Breeder is a free, fast Palworld breeding calculator. It covers all {{n_pals}} Pals and {{n_pairs}} parent combinations from Palworld v1.0.

Where the data comes from

  • Breeding combinations: the full v1.0 breeding table (every pair of the {{n_pals}} Pals, including subspecies and Terraria crossover Pals), cross-checked against palworld.kimpton.io and paldb.cc.
  • Partner skills: palworld.gg and paldb.cc, as of July 2026.
  • Passive skills: palworld.gg and paldeck.cc, as of July 2026.
  • Paldeck numbers and elements: paldb.cc.

Patches 1.0.1 through 1.0.5 (July to September 2026) did not add Pals or change breeding results. Last reviewed {{today}}.
