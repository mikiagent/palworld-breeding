          How breeding works in Palworld

          1. One male, one female

          Put a male and a female Pal in a Breeding Farm with a Cake. Which one is which gender does not change the child, except for Katress and Wixen.

          2. The child is fixed

          Every pair always produces the same species. Use the calculator above to check before you spend a Cake.

          3. Passives are inherited

          The child rolls its passive skills from both parents. See the passive skills list to pick what to breed for.
